Method Each letter is a real solid: the glyph contour is lifted out of the font file, closed into a shape with its counters as holes, and swept into a bevelled extrusion. The bevel is only a few hundredths of a unit wide and is given its own polished material, separate from the brushed face, because a wide soft edge catches nothing — it is the tight polished bevel that draws the hard line of specular travelling across the word as the camera arcs. What it reflects is a prefiltered room with actual light panels in it, not a gradient.
