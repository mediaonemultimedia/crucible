Method Nothing here is a baked lightmap. Every frame, a grid of photons is fired from the light toward the glass and traced in a vertex shader — refracted on entry, marched through the interior, refracted again on exit — then splatted as soft additive points into a floating-point texture, purely by landing where they land. Density does the rest: where photons converge, the texture gets bright. That texture is sampled back in the next pass, while the same glass is raymarched per pixel with its own two-bounce refraction for the object itself.
