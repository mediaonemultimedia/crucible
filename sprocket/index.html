Sprocket

Scroll engine 01

Raw scroll position is a target. A second value, virtual, eases toward that target every frame — virtual += (target − virtual) × rate, frame-rate independent. Every camera position, gear angle and set-piece state below reads only the virtual value, never the raw one. Drag this low and the lag becomes obvious; drag it high and the two converge almost instantly.

Playback 02

Space starts or stops an automatic scroll through the piece at a constant rate — a programmatic drive of the real scrollbar, not a separate playback mode. Scrolling manually stops it. R snaps straight back to the top.

Stations 03

Jump straight to a set-piece. Each jump moves the real scrollbar — the scene always renders the exact frame that scroll position maps to, whether you scrolled there by hand, by key, or by script.

Method 04

Named for the toothed wheel that feeds a film reel through a projector in exact lockstep with playback position. The camera's dolly, the gear's rotation, and every set-piece's state are pure functions of one number — the virtual scroll progress — so jumping anywhere renders precisely that frame. No mesh here either: rails, gear, reel, gate and lens are all one raymarched signed-distance field, smooth-min blended where Armature (No. 14) proved it.

Method Two numbers: raw (the page's real scrollTop, 0–1) and virtual (raw, eased). The shader only ever reads virtual — camera dolly, gear rotation, reel spin, gate glow, lens bloom. Jump anywhere and the scene snaps to that exact state; scroll normally and it glides.
