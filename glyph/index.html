Reveal 02

One animation, not two. Spread is the share of the take spent handing the rise from the first shape to the last — at zero the whole mark inflates together, near one it builds a piece at a time.

Room 03

A real modelled room, not a gradient — a mirror finish reflects shapes, and the rank of tubes in Vitrine is what makes two shapes at different heights read as being at different heights.

Exposure is measured, not assumed — the subject's own mean luminance is read off a render and bisected onto the band the series sits in. A white mark and a navy one do not want the same number, and a new file is exposed for on load.

Mark 04

Any SVG. Fills become base colours, fill-rule becomes holes, and paint order becomes the default Z ladder. Drop a file anywhere on the page.
