Method Nothing here is a function of time. A velocity field is held in GPU memory and pushed forward every frame by a chain of WGSL compute passes — forcing, semi-Lagrangian advection, curl, vorticity confinement. Advection alone makes the field compressible: it invents matter. So each frame the divergence is measured and an iterative Jacobi solve runs the pressure field to convergence, twenty-four passes over every cell, and the gradient is subtracted back out. What is left is incompressible — and the residual printed at left is the proof. The particles are not animated. They are integrated through that field, in a compute pass, and they remember where they have been.
