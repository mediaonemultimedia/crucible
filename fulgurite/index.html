Method A Laplace potential field is relaxed over the whole voxel grid every frame — not a box around the growing cluster, the real field, so real gradients form. The cluster sits at potential zero, the far boundary at one. Each step a candidate cell adjacent to the cluster is chosen at random, weighted by φη — the dielectric breakdown model, Niemeyer–Pietronero–Wiesmann. Raise η and the arcs sharpen into needles; lower it and they broaden into a bush. The glow is drawn from the tubes alone, in their own pass, before it ever touches the sphere.
