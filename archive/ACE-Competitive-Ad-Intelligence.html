05 · Recommended Moves

Five ways to close the gap.

Ranked by how directly each addresses a gap shown above.

1
Find a wedge Camrose Energy hasn't taken.
ACE and Camrose Energy occupy nearly the same quadrant. Camrose got there first with school-donation storytelling. ACE needs its own specific proof point — not another "we're local too" ad.
2
Fix attribution before scaling spend.
ACE's own 2026 audit found no tracking between Meta, applications, and Energize Alberta. Panel 2 above is a placeholder for real numbers — wiring up conversion tracking turns it from a planning guess into an actual dashboard.
3
Match Legacy Energy's rate framing, not just its rate.
Legacy Energy undercuts ACE by 0.19¢/kWh but wins more on specificity (named competitors, guaranteed savings, a $1,000 hook) than on price alone. ACE can out-frame this without a price war.
4
Scale variant count toward the 44-60 range.
Five variants can't sustain frequency against Legacy Energy's 60 or Easymax's 44. Use Panel 1's presets as a planning target, not a one-time launch.
5
ATCOenergy and Direct Energy are dark — don't defend against ghosts.
Zero real Meta presence from either. Budget and creative effort belongs against Legacy Energy, Camrose Energy, Easymax, and Encor, not the two dormant retailers.
