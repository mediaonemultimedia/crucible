# Pillow Fight — frame data

Generated by `node tools/framedata.mjs` from `src/fighters/index.js`. Ticks are 1/60 s. Reach is in sim units (1000 = one world unit, 9 cm at plush scale) from the fighter's middle, counting the ground a move covers before it hits (the heavy's lunge). Advantage is the attacker's, from the last active frame, ignoring hit-pause (it stops both sides alike); a knockdown is marked KD.

## Combat rules

- **Block is E.** Standing E blocks high and mid; S+E (crouching) blocks low and mid; overheads (jump attacks, the dive slam, Sky Neck, the Royal Squash) need a standing block. Holding back only walks away. Blocking holds you in place (no walking, jumping or dashing); every blocked hit pushes both fighters apart (the blocker 1.5× the hit's push, the attacker half of it — all of it in the corner). Only signatures, specials and shots chip.
- **Throw is O+P together** (pressed within 2 ticks of each other), at close range (touching + 520): startup 4, active 4–6, 30 ticks if it whiffs. It beats a blocker; it can't grab anyone in the air, knocked down, or still in hit/block stun. **Tech** it with O+P inside the first 12 ticks of the hold: both are pushed apart, no damage. The toss lands at frame 24 of the hold (a knockdown).
- **Counter-hit:** hitting someone in their move's startup does 125% damage and +8 ticks of hitstun (a COUNTER pop).
- **Chains:** on contact (hit or block), inside the cancel window (up to 9 ticks after the last active frame): light → light (after its active frames) / heavy / S+P / special; low → light / heavy / S+P; heavy → S+P, or the special on a full meter. Three lights in a string lock lights out for 26 ticks.
- **Amplified finisher:** a heavy / signature / special cancelled into out of a chain, landing on someone still in hitstun, does 132% (one link) or 144% (two or more), a hard knockdown, and builds +90 / +130 meter. AMPLIFIED! on the HUD.
- **Scaling:** the first three hits of a combo are full damage, then −15% a hit (to 45%; a special's hits not below 80%). The same light landing again within 2 s does 85%, 70%, 55%.
- **Whiffs:** a heavy that touches nothing adds 6 ticks to its recovery.
- **K.O.:** a 6-tick freeze, then the launch at 25% speed for 90 ticks and back to full over 18, a bounce, the banner at tick 128 (150 ticks in all); the match-winning K.O.: 120 slow ticks, 190 in all, higher.

Every fighter: forward dash (double-tap forward) 16 ticks, 2040 units; backdash (double-tap back) 14 ticks, 1190 units (approximate: the last ticks ease off).
