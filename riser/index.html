Method Every particle carries a temperature and a volume ratio, not a fixed shape. Each frame they scatter mass and momentum onto a background grid in a WGSL compute pass, the grid resolves pressure, gravity and mold contact, and the particles gather it back — Material Point Method, liquid and solid living in the same solve. The grid is what makes them a liquid rather than a crowd: eight particles share every cell, so each one's quadratic kernel overlaps its neighbours and the whole body moves as one material. Cooling drifts a particle from viscous fluid toward locked solid as it crosses the liquidus. Nothing here is a mesh. The surface is reconstructed every frame from the point cloud — camera-facing spheres whose depth is blurred at the scale of a particle and relit as one continuous body of metal.
