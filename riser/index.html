Method Every particle carries a temperature and a deformation state, not a fixed shape. Each frame they scatter mass and momentum onto a MAC grid in a WGSL compute pass, the grid resolves gravity and mold contact, and the particles gather it back — Material Point Method, liquid and solid living in the same solve. Cooling drifts a particle's stiffness from viscous fluid toward locked solid as it crosses a threshold. Nothing here is a mesh. The surface is reconstructed every frame from a cloud of points — camera-facing spheres, their depth bilateral-blurred and relit as one continuous body of metal.
