Inside the experiment 05

The body is a position-based soft body: a shape-matched point cloud for the head (stuffing is how hard it springs back to shape) and eight arms of overlapping three-point shape-matched windows, held by long-range tethers so they stretch but never snap.

The fur is the body drawn 64 times, each shell pushed a little further out and cut into strands by a jittered cell pattern. Each strand bends with a groom map you paint with the comb, trails the surface on a damped spring, and is lit along its own axis (Kajiya-Kay), so brushed patches catch light like velvet.

Arms near the stick reach for it, then wrap from the tip on a helix around the wood. Pull harder than the grip and they let go.
