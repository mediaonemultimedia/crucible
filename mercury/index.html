Motion 02

k breathes on a slow sine. As it widens the separate bodies lose their boundaries and the mass reads as one form condensing; as it narrows they differentiate again.

Structure 03

Cost is count × march steps per pixel — every step of every ray evaluates every sphere. At 768 distance evaluations per primary ray, doubling the count doubles the shader load.

Method There is no mesh. Every pixel sphere-traces a distance field of eight bodies welded by a polynomial smooth-minimum, so the surface between them is a real analytic blend rather than two spheres touching. What it reflects is a modelled room — the reflection ray intersects an actual ceiling grid with dark voids in it, a lit backdrop on one side and a black quarter on the other. That asymmetry is the whole trick: a gradient gives you a shiny ball, structured geometry gives you metal. The ray then bounces a second time, so the mass sees itself.
